## My Technology Leadership Focus

- **Strategy and solutioning:** Translate operational pain points into clear requirements and workable delivery scopes.
- **Architecture and integration:** Choose appropriate cloud, data, application, and integration patterns.
- **Cybersecurity and governance:** Build in access boundaries, evidence, accountability, and careful handling of sensitive information.
- **Execution and validation:** Use phased implementation and testable acceptance criteria rather than technology claims alone.
- **Business value:** Favor usable workflows, operational transparency, and maintainability over unnecessary complexity.
